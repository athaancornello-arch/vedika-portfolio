About me

Hi, I'm Vedika, a creative soul who turns ordinary moments into something beautiful.

I'm drawn to aesthetics, photography, fashion, lifestyle, and the little details that make a story unforgettable. Whether it's creating a reel, capturing a moment, or experimenting with new visual ideas, I love expressing myself through creativity.

I believe every frame has a feeling, every color tells a story, and every creative idea deserves its own space to shine.

This little corner of the internet is where my ideas, inspirations, and creative world come together. Take a look around, explore my work, and get to know the girl behind the frames. ♡

My creative process

From a feeling to a finished frame.

Every piece starts the same way, with something small worth keeping.

  1. 1

    Notice

    I start with the small things: a patch of light, a color pairing, a feeling worth keeping.

  2. 2

    Collect

    References go onto a mood board: palettes, poses, textures and songs that belong to the story.

  3. 3

    Frame

    I choose the light, the angle and the outfit, and decide what the frame should feel like.

  4. 4

    Edit

    Color, rhythm and cuts. Reels get timed to the music, photos stay warm, soft and true to the moment.

  5. 5

    Share

    I post it, listen to what people feel, and keep making the ones that make them stay.
